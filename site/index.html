Welcome

Free AI Coding for Beginners

A plain-English, step-by-step manual for learning to code with AI — completely free, with no account required for the models we recommend.

Written in the spirit of Dylan Asdale's The Massive AI Manual: AI explained "like a friend would," never condescending, always practical.
